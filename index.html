Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Select a category below to explore detailed answers regarding our digital lending platform.

1. What is Stackly and how does it work?

Stackly is a next-generation digital lending platform that connects borrowers directly with premier scheduled banks and NBFCs, streamlining the entire funding lifecycle through automated technology and instant verification APIs.

2. Who is eligible to apply for a loan or credit line?

Any resident citizen aged 21 or older with a stable source of income (salaried, self-employed, or business owner) and a valid national tax identifier or government-issued ID is eligible to apply.

3. Are there any hidden charges or sign-up fees?

No. We maintain a 100% transparent fee structure. All applicable interest rates, processing fees, and terms are fully disclosed before you sign your loan agreement.

4. Can I use Stackly for business financing?

Yes, we offer specialized commercial credit lines designed for inventory expansion, working capital management, and multi-currency international vendor disbursements.

5. How can I contact customer support if I face issues?

Our 24/7 digital concierge is available via live chat inside your dashboard, or you can email our support team directly at support@stackly-finance.com.

1. How long does the approval process take?

Most loan applications are evaluated, verified, and approved in under 5 minutes using our proprietary automated credit scoring algorithm.

2. What documents are required for digital KYC?

You only need a valid government-issued ID, PAN/tax card, proof of address, and recent bank statements which can be securely shared via our digital integration partners.

3. Will checking my eligibility impact my credit score?

No, initial pre-qualification checks use a soft credit pull, which has absolutely zero impact on your credit score.

4. When is the loan amount disbursed to my bank account?

Once your digital agreement is signed, funds are immediately transferred to your designated bank account, usually arriving within 15 to 30 minutes.

5. Can I modify my loan amount after submitting the form?

Yes, you can adjust your requested loan amount or tenure slider during the pre-approval review stage before final contract confirmation.

1. Are there any prepayment penalties for clearing early?

Never. You can make high-value partial pre-payments or close your entire loan early at any time with zero penalty fees.

2. How are monthly EMI installments collected?

Installments are automatically debited on your chosen due date through an automated electronic NACH or digital mandate set up during onboarding.

3. What happens if I miss a scheduled payment due date?

A nominal late fee applies for delayed payments, and prolonged defaults may be reported to credit bureaus, affecting future loan approvals.

4. Can I request a tenure extension if I experience cash flow issues?

Yes, eligible borrowers can request flexible tenure extensions or restructuring options directly through their customer dashboard.

5. How do I download my repayment schedule and tax certificates?

You can instantly view, download, or print your complete amortization schedule and digital No Objection Certificates (NOC) directly from your portal.

1. Is my personal and financial data secure?

Yes, we use military-grade 256-bit SSL encryption for all data in transit and at rest, complying with top-tier international security frameworks.

2. Do you sell or share my information with third-party marketers?

We adhere to a strict Zero Data Selling policy. Your records are never distributed or sold to advertising brokers or external commercial entities.

3. Are you compliant with national digital lending regulations?

All lending partners integrated on our platform are fully licensed, regulated, and compliant with national banking and consumer protection laws.

4. How can I request the deletion of my account data?

You can submit a data privacy and account purge request through your account settings or by contacting our data protection officer directly.

5. What authentication measures protect my account access?

We enforce multi-factor authentication (MFA), secure OTP verifications for sensitive transactions, and automated session timeouts to safeguard your account.
