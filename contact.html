The Institutional Standard

Our advisory framework is built on absolute clarity. Review our core guidelines below or dispatch a direct inquiry through our secure channels.

01

What is the typical disbursement timeline for business loans?

Once all institutional verification documents and collateral valuations are successfully completed at our Salem headquarters, funds are typically disbursed within 48 to 72 business hours directly to your designated corporate account.

02

What documents are required for personal and home loans?

You will need standard government-issued photo ID, recent proof of income (salary slips or audited IT returns for the past 2 years), bank statements for the last 6 months, and valid property title deeds for home loan applications.

03

Can I schedule an in-person consultation at the Salem office?

Yes, absolutely. You can schedule a private executive consultation by clicking our office visit scheduler or contacting our WhatsApp concierge directly to reserve a private meeting suite in Tower 4.

04

Are there any hidden prepayment or foreclosure penalties?

Stackly maintains a strict policy of financial transparency. Prepayment terms are customized based on your loan structure, with zero penalties on floating-rate corporate credit lines after the initial lock-in period.
